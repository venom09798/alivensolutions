Who We Are

Your Trusted Digital Partner

Aliven Solutions is a full service digital agency based in Berrien Springs, Michigan, USA. We specialize in Web Development, Application Development, Video Editing, Graphic Design, Digital Marketing, PPC, SEO, AI Services and Animation.

Our team combines creativity technical expertise and strategic thinking to help businesses of all sizes establish a powerful online presence and achieve measurable results.

We believe in transparency quality and long term partnerships. Every project we take on is treated with the same dedication as if it were our own brand.
